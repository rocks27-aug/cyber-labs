WEEK 3 — NOTES

Interface:
`ip link` checks interfaces and link state. The lab showed eth0 as UP and LOWER_UP.

Routing:
`ip route get <destination>` shows the route selected for a particular destination.
Observed gateway: 10.0.2.2
Interface: eth0
Source IP: 10.0.2.15

Ports:
`ss -tulpn` showed TCP port 22 listening through sshd, PID 18635.

Processes:
`pgrep`, `ps` and `ps -fp <PID>` identify and inspect running processes.

Services:
`systemctl status ssh` showed SSH had started successfully and later became inactive/dead. The journal recorded signal 15 and successful deactivation.

DNS:
`/etc/resolv.conf` contained 172.20.1.15, 172.20.1.5 and 8.8.8.8 as configured DNS servers.
`dig www.google.com` successfully returned multiple A records using DNS server 172.20.1.15.

Public DNS observation:
1.1.1.1 DNS query timed out, ping showed 100% packet loss, and TCP/443 returned Connection refused. The local gateway 10.0.2.2 remained reachable. These are test-specific observations and do not by themselves prove that the Internet is unavailable.

HTTP:
`curl -I https://google.com` returned HTTP/2 301 and redirected to https://www.google.com/.

Troubleshooting principle:
Do not rely on one test. Correlate interface, route, port, process, service, DNS, connectivity, HTTP and logs.
